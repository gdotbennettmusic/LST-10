Privacy Policy

Loopology™ Session Timer (Android app and web version)
Developer: gdotbennettmusic
Effective date: October 18, 2026

The short version

Your session data stays on your device. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. The developer does not collect, receive, sell, or share your personal information.

What the app stores, and where

The timer saves your sessions (session IDs, titles, start/stop timestamps, pauses, notes, running totals), your settings (theme, colors, alerts, clock format, session-ID prefix), and any screenshots or files you choose to attach. All of this is kept in the app's local storage on your device. It is never sent to the developer or to any server operated by the developer.

If you uninstall the app or clear its data, this information is deleted from your device. Use CSV export first if you want to keep a copy.

Files you import or export

Import PDF / CSV: files you pick are read on your device only. PDF reading uses a copy of PDF.js bundled inside the app, so nothing is uploaded.
CSV download and sharing: when you export or share, Android's share sheet opens and you choose where the file goes (for example Files, Google Drive, or email). After that, the app or service you choose handles the file under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): used only to show your target-length alert on your device.
  • Keep screen awake: used only while a session is running, if you turn it on.
  • Vibration: short alert vibration.
  • Internet / Google Play Billing: used only to check for and process the optional one-time "Pro" upgrade through Google Play, and to open links you tap. The timer itself works fully offline.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is processed entirely by Google Play. The developer does not receive your card or payment details. Google's handling of purchase information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted with a new effective date.
